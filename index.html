About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical, Computer and Energy Engineering at Arizona State University and a member of the Performance and Resource Optimization in Networks (PROTON Lab), advised by Dr. Eirini Eleni Tsiropoulou.

My research focuses on how autonomous drones and wireless networks can learn to make decisions under uncertainty. I develop multi-agent learning methods, including distributed regret learning and reinforcement learning, that let UAV swarms coordinate, localize themselves, and perform wireless sensing without GPS or centralized control. I also work on AI-native integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and on low altitude economy applications, from drone scheduling to LLM-driven networks enabled by over-the-air computation.

Before starting my Ph.D., I spent nearly four years as a software engineer at Spotawheel, where I grew into a lead role, and I earned a joint BSc & MEng in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA).
